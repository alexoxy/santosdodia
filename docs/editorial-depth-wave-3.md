# SantosDia — Editorial Depth Wave 3

Updated: 2026-08-30

## Purpose

This selective wave reviews the nine remaining profiles below the internal editorial-depth target after Waves 1 and 2. Its objective is not to force a numerical score. It adds explanatory value only where the existing reviewed evidence supports a clearer distinction between history, Scripture, hagiography, ecclesial recognition and calendar observance.

The chronology and initial product tools remain complete and available. Product availability, search indexability and monetization remain separate layers.

## Editorial method

The additions are SantosDia-authored synthesis over the evidence and source categories already attached to each biography. They are deliberately focused on evidentiary boundaries:

- canonical Scripture is not silently expanded by later tradition;
- historical identity is not created by a calendar entry;
- apocryphal and hagiographical material remains labelled by its source category;
- a Church-specific title or recognition is not presented as universal Christian status;
- a source competent for one Church does not silently validate another Church's calendar;
- one canonical Person can relate to multiple Observances and Occurrences without identity duplication;
- similar names do not establish identity.

## Measurement rule

The repository audit remains the judge of the internal depth target. The target after this wave may be 29/29 deep-ready, but the threshold will not be weakened and filler will not be added merely to reach that number. If a profile remains below the target after a useful evidence-boundary addition, it remains below the target until further substantive evidence or editorial value justifies additional work.

The internal target is not a Google or AdSense word-count requirement.

## Publication safety

- no new saint identities or public URLs;
- no D1 writes;
- no copied, translated, shortened or lightly paraphrased third-party prose;
- existing provenance remains attached to the underlying biography records;
- Today, Calendar, Search, Calculator, ICS/webcal and APIs remain intact;
- search indexation remains governed by the existing editorial gates;
- AdSense serving remains disabled while the site is in `REMEDIATION_REQUIRED` state.
